Founded in 1890 in neighboring Fellbach near Stuttgart, AMF is a global pioneer in industrial clamping technology, zero-point workholding systems, and modular CNC automation. With over 5,000 products—from mechanical and hydraulic clamping elements to tool-changer grippers and wireless sensoring—AMF helps manufacturers maximize setup efficiency and precision. Technology Partner · Drive Technology

MÄDLER GmbH

Headquartered right in Stuttgart since 1882, MÄDLER GmbH is a leading manufacturer and supplier of mechanical drive technology and power transmission components. Their extensive catalog of over 30,000 parts—spanning timing belts, pulleys, spur and bevel gears, couplings, bearings, and linear guides—powers engineering and robotics systems across Europe.

Standard Elements Partner · Ganter Norm

Otto Ganter GmbH & Co. KG

Founded in 1894 in Furtwangen in the Black Forest, Otto Ganter GmbH & Co. KG is the global market leader in standard machine and operating elements. Providing over 80,000 DIN, ISO, and proprietary Ganter Norm variants—from indexing plungers and clamping levers to ergonomic handles and vibration mounts—Ganter delivers industrial precision backed by full 3D CAD data.
